CPU introduction
------------------

- i.MX28 series of multi-media application processor is the latest member of Freescale ARM9 series of items.i.MX28 series of items combine unique display,power management and connection speciaty based on ARM9,reduce the cost and complexity of system for the application sensitive to the budget.
- Tool and software easily used will facilitate you designing unique products in terms of industrial control and consumer electronics in a shorter period of time, i.MX28 features optimized function and power consumption,which is very suitable to be used in the systems without fans and portable devices powered by battery. It provide multiple options for connection functions,including dual 10/100 ethernet(support IEEE?1588) based on L2 exchange function.which can meet the special demands in industrial application,moreover,its connection functions of CAN,USB and ethernet can completely meet the demands in automotive industry.
- i.MX28 care about the overall cost of system, it combines physical USB interface(PHY),10/100 ethernet,power management and displayed controller in resistance touch screen,so it can provide economic and highly effective solutions for products in automotive grade.e.g it can used for monitor in highway toll station,,CAN gateway,accessory of automotive audio)USB or SD card adaptor) and car radio controlled by touch screen.designer can lay out many creative and visual user interfaces through LCD controller with touch screen.

CPU characteristic
---------------------

|  1) 454MHz ARM926EJ-S kernel with 16KB/32KB cache.
|  2) PMU carry highly effective on-chip DC/DC, support li-ion battery
|  3) Dual 10/100 l IEEE ® 1588 ethernet，provide support for RMII and L2 exchange function
|  4) Dual CAN interface
|  5) LCD controller with touch screen
|  6) NAND support – SLC/MLC and eMMC 4.4 manageable.
|  7) Hardware BCH (up to 20 bit correction )
|  8) 200 MHz 16 位 DDR2, LV-DDR2, mDDR supported by external storage.
|  9) Carry embed PHY dual high speed USB.
|  10) 8 universal 12 bit ADC channel and one 2 Msps ADC channel.
|  11) With temperature sensor, to provide thermal protection.
|  12) A few kinds of connection interface (UARTs, SSP, SDIO, SPI, I2C, I2S)
|  13) 3.3V I/O，10 years of service life (industrial control).
|  14) Package and temperature
 | 289 BGA 14x14mm .8mm
 | -40C to +85C (industrial control)
|  15) These items are included in long term supply by Freescale,who guarantee at least 15 years' supply from the day one of marketing.
|  16) Core board adopt stamp hole to guarantee stability and credability of high speed signal and ensure a nice connectivity,which can withstand violent shock and can completely meet the test of violent shock in harsh environment for industry and automobile.
