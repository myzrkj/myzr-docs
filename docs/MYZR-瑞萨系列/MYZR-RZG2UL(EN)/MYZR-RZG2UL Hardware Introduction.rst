Baseboard Schematic Design Description
----------------------------------------

Main Power Supply Circuit
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

| The baseboard power is supplied by a 5V DC power source, which is introduced through the DC-005 socket (J1).

.. figure:: /image/MYZR-瑞萨系列/MYZR-RZG2UL/Myzr_rzg2ul_power1.png
   :alt: Myzr_rzg2ul_power1.png

.. figure:: /image/MYZR-瑞萨系列/MYZR-RZG2UL/Myzr_rzg2ul_power2.png
   :alt: Myzr_rzg2ul_power2.png

| After passing through a self-recovering fuse and a 5.6V overvoltage protection circuit, the 5V power is stepped down to 3.6V via a DCDC converter to supply power to the core board. After the core board starts up, it controls the 5V-to-3.3V conversion on the baseboard through the MainPWR_EN signal. This ensures that the core board starts first before powering other components on the baseboard, preventing the occurrence of latch-up effects. The power consumption of the RZG2L_MB200 development board varies under different states. In actual tests, when equipped with the EC20-4G module and the company's RGB touch display, the maximum startup current exceeds 1.2A. In practical use, it is recommended to select a 5V switching power adapter with an output current of not less than 3A.

RTC Real-Time Clock Circuit
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. figure:: /image/MYZR-瑞萨系列/MYZR-RZG2UL/Myzr_rzg2ul_rtc.png
   :alt: Myzr_rzg2ul_rtc.png

| The RTC chip used in this circuit has a built-in crystal matching capacitor. If you need to replace the solution, pay attention to the crystal accuracy. Matching capacitors can be connected in parallel between the two crystal networks and ground to adjust the accuracy. When the baseboard is powered on, the 3.3V power on the baseboard supplies power to the RTC chip and charges the battery BT1; when the baseboard is powered off, the battery BT1 discharges to serve as the power source for the RTC chip.

Ethernet Interface Circuit
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. figure:: /image/MYZR-瑞萨系列/MYZR-RZG2UL/963px-Myzr_rzg2ul_ethernet1.png
   :alt: 963px-Myzr_rzg2ul_ethernet1.png

   :alt: Myzr_rzg2ul_ethernet2.png

| To support Ethernet functionality, an Ethernet port chip must be added to the baseboard and connected via an RJ45 interface. It should be noted that the two indicator lights of the RJ45 interface must be designed in accordance with this schematic. Note: During PCB design, the 4 groups of Ethernet signal lines must be routed in accordance with differential rules and have equal lengths within each differential pair. A spacing of more than 3 times the line width must be maintained between differential pairs and other networks; the equal-length error within a differential pair must be within 5mil, and the equal-length error between differential pairs must be within 25mil.

USB HOST Circuit for USB Network Expansion
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. figure:: /image/MYZR-瑞萨系列/MYZR-RZG2UL/Myzr_rzg2ul_usbhost1.png
   :alt: Myzr_rzg2ul_usbhost1.png

| This circuit expands one USB interface of the core board into 4 USB interfaces. During PCB design, the 90Ohm/DLM11SN900HY2L common-mode filter should be placed close to the chip. Each group of USB signal lines must be routed in accordance with differential rules and have equal lengths within each differential pair. A spacing of more than 3 times the line width should be maintained between differential pairs and other networks as much as possible, and the equal-length error within a differential pair must be within 5mil. The USB HOST chip has two USB outputs connected to USB-A female sockets, which can be used to connect external USB flash drives or mice. See the following diagram for the circuit:

.. figure:: /image/MYZR-瑞萨系列/MYZR-RZG2UL/Myzr_rzg2ul_usbhost2.png
   :alt: Myzr_rzg2ul_usbhost2.png

| In the diagram, E1 is the control & protection chip for the 5V output power of the USB interface. U6 controls two power channels in total, and the control signal for each channel comes from the PRTPWR and OCS networks of the corresponding channel of the USB HOST chip. As shown in the following diagram, the remaining peripheral circuits of the USB HOST chip should be designed completely with reference to the schematic of this development board. During PCB design, the power network traces of the chip should be widened, and power decoupling capacitors should be placed close to the chip pins; the crystal should be placed close to the chip, and the crystal network should be kept away from other signal lines as much as possible with ground shielding. Ground shielding is required around the crystal itself.

.. figure:: /image/MYZR-瑞萨系列/MYZR-RZG2UL/Myzr_rzg2ul_usbhost3.png

MINI_PCIE - 4G Module Circuit
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. figure:: /image/MYZR-瑞萨系列/MYZR-RZG2UL/963px-Myzr_rzg2ul_4g.png
   :alt: 963px-Myzr_rzg2ul_4g.png

| This circuit is designed based on the EC20-4G module and adopts the PCI Express Mini Card 1.2 standard interface. In actual use, it communicates with the CPU via the USB network. The theoretical maximum current required by the module can exceed 2A. Therefore, during PCB design, the power traces must be widened, and power decoupling capacitors should be placed close to the interface pins. The total capacitance of the capacitors is recommended to be greater than 470uF. The USB signal lines must be routed in accordance with differential rules and have equal lengths within each differential pair. A spacing of more than 3 times the line width should be maintained between differential pairs and other networks as much as possible, and the equal-length error within a differential pair must be within 5mil. D6 is the working status indicator of the module; CON3 is the SIM card socket, which should be placed close to the 4G module. The network between the module and the SIM card should be routed in groups to avoid excessive length errors caused by too long traces, and should be kept away from strong signal interference sources and traces.

RGB Touch Display Circuit
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. figure:: /image/MYZR-瑞萨系列/MYZR-RZG2UL/Myzr_rzg2ul_rgb.png
   :alt: Myzr_rzg2ul_rgb.png

| This circuit is designed based on the company's 7-inch RGB touch display interface, which is powered by a single 5V supply and has a peak current of up to 1A. The display uses the RGB888 bus for communication, and the touch screen uses the IIC bus interface for communication. During PCB design, the networks of the RGB888 bus must be routed with equal lengths between the core board and the interface, and the line spacing must meet the 3W rule requirement; the IIC bus must be routed in groups to avoid excessive length errors caused by too long traces.

WIFI & Bluetooth Module Circuit
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. figure:: /image/MYZR-瑞萨系列/MYZR-RZG2UL/Myzr_rzg2ul_wifi.png
   :alt: Myzr_rzg2ul_wifi.png

| This development board communicates with the WIFI & Bluetooth module via USB. During PCB design, the USB signal lines must be routed in accordance with differential rules and have equal lengths within each differential pair; the network where the antenna interface E1 (in the diagram) is located requires trace design that meets 50Ω impedance. The traces should be as short as possible without right-angle bends, and ground shielding is required around them to prevent signal interference.

Audio Circuit
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. figure:: /image/MYZR-瑞萨系列/MYZR-RZG2UL/963px-Myzr_rzg2ul_audio1.png
   :alt: 963px-Myzr_rzg2ul_audio1.png

.. figure:: /image/MYZR-瑞萨系列/MYZR-RZG2UL/Myzr_rzg2ul_audio2.png
   :alt: Myzr_rzg2ul_audio2.png

| The baseboard uses the WM8960 chip as its hardware audio chip, and the following descriptions of the audio chip all apply to this WM8960 chip. The audio chip outputs stereo audio signals to headphones and speakers, and receives mono audio signals from the microphone. P12 is the built-in Class D power amplifier output terminal of the WM8960 audio chip. When an 8Ω speaker is connected, the output power is 1W; when a 4Ω speaker is connected, the output power is 2W. Note: The speaker power comes from the Class D power amplifier, not the traditional analog power amplifier. A speaker should be connected to two adjacent pins of the socket; the two speakers cannot share wires, nor can the speaker be connected to the ground wire. If the user needs to connect an external power amplifier, the signal can only be obtained from the headphone socket, not from the speaker interface. The audio function requires an external clock to operate. During PCB design, the I2S0 signal and the Audio_SCL, Audio_SDA network groups must be routed in groups to avoid excessive length errors caused by overly long traces. The traces for analog signals such as those from the microphone and headphones must be widened to more than 10mil, with smooth routing. Ground shielding is recommended, and the traces should be kept away from signal lines and other sources of interference.

OTG Circuit
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. figure:: /image/MYZR-瑞萨系列/MYZR-RZG2UL/Myzr_rzg2ul_otg.png
   :alt: Myzr_rzg2ul_otg.png

| The OTG function uses a TYPE-C interface. The pull-up resistors for the ID pin and other pins should be greater than 30kΩ; when using this group of USB signals, a 3.3V voltage must be input to USB0_VBUSIN. U7 is the control & protection chip for the 5V output power of the OTG-USB interface. During PCB design, the USB networks must strictly follow the differential equal-length routing rules.
