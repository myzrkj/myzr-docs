Startup Manual
================

Development Board Connection
-------------------------------

1. Check the Power Switch

|  Toggle the development board's power switch "POW_KEY" to the "OFF" side to ensure the development board's power switch is in the disconnected state.

2. Connect the Serial Cable

|  Connect one end of the Type-C cable to the "Debug" port of the development board, and the other end to the PC's serial port.
|  Refer to the :doc:`《Xshell Reference Manual》 </docs/COMMON/Xshell.RM.参考手册>` to create a new serial session and open the session, Set the baud rate to '1500000'.

3. Connect the Download Cable

|  Connect one end of the Type-C cable to U5, and the other end to the rear USB port of the PC.

4. Connect the Power Cable

|  Connect one end of the 12V-2A power adapter to the "J1" of the development board, and the other end to a mains power (220V AC) socket.

Start the Development Board
-----------------------------

|  Toggle the development board's power switch "POW_KEY" to the "ON" side to turn on the development board's power. After the system startup is completed, the development board will log in automatically.
