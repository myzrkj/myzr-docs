Platform Introduction
========================

Product Overview
-------------------

* Allwinner H618 is a cost-effective multimedia processor designed for smart TV boxes, smart display terminals, educational tablets, and edge computing devices. Based on the quad-core Cortex-A53 architecture, it integrates the Mali-G31 MP2 GPU, supporting 8K@24fps H.265 video decoding and 4K@25fps H.264 video encoding. Equipped with a rich set of audio and video interfaces, it can be applied to product forms such as OTT (Over-The-Top) devices and smart screens. It mainly targets fields including smart terminals, multimedia processing, the Internet of Things (IoT), and consumer electronics.


Target Applications
----------------------

* Smart TVs and Set-Top Boxes (STBs): Support 4K HDR video playback and hardware decoding for streaming services (e.g., Netflix, YouTube). Compatible with the Android TV system, integrating voice assistants and intelligent recommendation functions.
* Tablets and Educational Devices: Enable multitasking, touch interaction, e-book reading, and online education applications.
* Industrial Control and IoT Gateways: Realize device control, data acquisition, and edge computing through the Linux system. Support the expansion of industrial interfaces such as RS485/232 and CAN bus.
* Smart Home Control Centers: Support voice control (with integrated microphone array) and security monitoring (camera input + AI face recognition).
* Commercial Displays and Advertising Players: Enable multi-screen interaction, remote content management, and high-resolution advertisement playback.


Key Features
---------------

* 4-core Arm Cortex-A53
* Supports up to 8K@24fps H.265 decoding and 4K@25fps H.264 encoding
* Supports up to 4K@60fps HDMI and TV CVBS OUT display output
* Supports HDR10, HDR10+, HLG, and HDR Vivid
