Product Introduction
======================

1 Product Overview
--------------------

1.1 Product Brief
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

|  GW305 is a wireless communication gateway applied in the industrial field. It provides isolated RS485 / isolated RS232 / 100Mbps Ethernet / optocoupler isolated DI / optocoupler isolated DO / relay switch interfaces to realize external device control, data collection, data parsing and transparent transmission. It can connect to servers or the cloud via 4G or Ethernet uplink to control terminal devices and collect information. The device meets industrial-grade standards in terms of temperature rating, electrostatic protection, EMC, vibration and other indicators, adapting to various harsh industrial environments and realizing highly reliable data transmission.

.. figure:: /image/MYZR-其他/网关/GW300/576px-GW300_1.1.0.1_1.png
   :alt: 576px-GW300_1.1.0.1_1.png

|  Device Connection:
|  Ethernet Port: Can be used as a WAN port to connect the gateway to the target network, or configured as a LAN port.
|  RS232/RS485: Used to connect external devices and support data collection, transparent transmission and data parsing.


1.2 Product Features
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

|  Operating temperature ranges from -20°C to 75°C. It supports 12V dual power input with multiple protection functions including overvoltage protection, overcurrent protection, reverse connection prevention, surge immunity and electrostatic protection. The power input can withstand 4KV lightning surge impact. The shell is made of 0.8mm high-quality electrolytic aluminum plate stamped by a dedicated metal mold, featuring exquisite appearance, good mechanical strength and electromagnetic interference resistance. It complies with industrial standards for electrostatic surge, EMC and vibration. Adopting DIN rail mounting, it is easy for on-site installation. The LAN, RS485 and RS232 interfaces ensure transmission reliability in industrial scenarios. A built-in RTC (Real-Time Clock) with an onboard backup battery supports high-performance big data industrial application scenarios.

**1.2.2 High Performance**

|  Equipped with Cortex-A35 quad-core processor with a main frequency up to 1.4GHz.

**1.2.3 4G Network Access**

|  Supports 802.11a/b/g/n/ac 2x2 protocol. It adopts dual-stream wireless LAN connection under 802.11n with a transmission rate up to 867Mbps. Supporting 2.4G and 5G dual bands, it features seamless roaming, high security, strong anti-interference capability and superior stability.

**1.2.4 Rich Data Communication Interfaces**

|  RS485 Interface: 3 channels with 4KV lightning protection
|  RS232 Interface: 2 channels with 4KV lightning protection
|  Ethernet Interface: 2 channels of 100M Ethernet with 4KV lightning protection
|  DI Interface: 1 channel
|  DO Interface: 1 channel
|  Relay Switch: 2 channels
